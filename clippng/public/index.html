Questions, answered.

How uploads, invoices and estimates work in clippng.in.

How does clipping work?

Upload a video, then press Clip it. The server reads the audio, finds the loudest moments and cuts each one into its own vertical MP4 file, ranked by an energy score. Turn on Auto-clip to skip the button. Choose how many clips, how long and which format before you start.

How do I download a clip?

Select a clip to open it on the phone, then press Download clip under the phone. Every clip tile has its own Download button, and each upload has a Download all button that gives you a zip.

How do I add a video?

Drag a video file onto the upload card or press Upload file. MP4, MOV, M4V, WEBM, MKV and AVI files work.

Can I paste a link?

Only when link import is turned on for your server, which needs yt-dlp installed. Otherwise upload the video file itself.

How do invoices work?

Fill in your details and your client's. Add line items by hand or press Add from clips to turn your clips into line items. Tax, discount and totals update live. Save the invoice, mark it Draft, Sent or Paid, and download it as a PDF.

Where is my data stored?

Uploads, clips and saved invoices live on the server that runs this app. Uploads and clips are deleted automatically after a set time, 24 hours unless your server changes it.

Are the earning estimates guaranteed?

No. They are illustrative ranges based on views, region and platform. Real payouts vary with your audience, the platform's rules and account verification.
